# HR Analytics: Attrition & WorkForce Trends
📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive HR Analytics dashboard analyzing employee attrition and workforce trends using a dataset of 1,470 employees. The objective is to identify key attrition drivers, understand workforce demographics, and generate data-driven insights that support improved employee retention and organizational health.

By examining factors such as overtime, job roles, business travel, marital status, age, and work-life balance, the analysis highlights which employee segments are most vulnerable to attrition and why employees leave the organization.


🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall employee attrition trends
Identify high-risk attrition factors
Understand demographic and departmental workforce patterns
Support HR decision-making through actionable insights


📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Employees: 1,470
Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%
Average Tenure: 7.01 Years
Average Monthly Income: 6.50K
Overtime Attrition Rate: 30.53%


🔍 Key Insights
1️⃣ Primary Attrition Drivers

Overtime Impact: Employees working overtime show a significantly higher attrition rate (30.53%) compared to non-overtime employees.

Job Roles:
Sales Representatives: 28.09% (Highest)
Laboratory Technicians: 16.91%
Human Resources: 16.31%
Business Travel: Employees who travel frequently exhibit the highest likelihood of attrition.
Commute Distance: Attrition spikes notably when the distance from home exceeds 20 miles.

2️⃣ Demographic Trends

Age: Highest attrition observed among employees aged 18–22, with attrition stabilizing as age increases.

Marital Status & Gender:
Single Males show the highest attrition (~25–28%).
Divorced Females show the lowest attrition.

Departmental Attrition:
Sales: 20.6% (Highest)
HR: 19.05%

3️⃣ Professional Growth & Work-Life Balance

Performance vs Salary Hike: A clear linear relationship exists between performance ratings and salary increments.

Work-Life Balance:
60.75% of employees rate it as “Better”
10.41% rate it as “Poor”, indicating a key attrition risk segment

💡 Strategic Recommendations

Workload Optimization: Review staffing and workload distribution to reduce the 30.53% attrition among overtime employees.
Role-Based Retention: Design targeted retention strategies for Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians.
Early Career Engagement: Introduce mentorship and career development programs for employees under 25 years.
Travel Policy Review: Reduce frequent travel through remote collaboration tools to improve retention.
